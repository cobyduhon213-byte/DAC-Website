The Founders

Elder Watson Diggs, Byron K. Armstrong, John M. Lee, Henry T. Asher, Marcus P. Blakemore, Guy L. Grant, Paul W. Caine, George W. Edmonds, Ezra D. Alexander, and Edward G. Irvin — ten students at Indiana University who founded the fraternity on January 5, 1911.

Objectives

  1. 1

    Unite college men of good character in a lasting bond of brotherhood.

  2. 2

    Encourage real, honorable achievement in every field of endeavor.

  3. 3

    Support members' spiritual, social, intellectual, and moral growth.

  4. 4

    Support the mission and goals of colleges and universities.

  5. 5

    Inspire service in the public interest.
