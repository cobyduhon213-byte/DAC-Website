Kappa Tower

Four decades of affordable senior housing, built and run by chapter members.

← History Denver Alumni History Kappa Tower Fraternity History Founders & Objectives Middle Western Province

Kappa Housing Inc. and Kappa Management Inc. — nonprofits founded by chapter members — have built and run affordable senior housing in Denver for four decades, now across two buildings and counting.

  • Kappa Tower I

    2160 Downing Street, Denver — opened 1984. 45 units across five stories, for low-income seniors.

  • Kappa Tower II

    9020 E Northfield Blvd, Denver — opened 2021 under Kappa Housing Board Chairman Larry A. Williams. 70 units for seniors 62 and up.

  • Kappa Tower III

    A second phase at the Northfield site (roughly 30 more units) was in planning as of Kappa Housing's last public update — confirm current status before publishing.
