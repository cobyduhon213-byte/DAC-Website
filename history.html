From Bloomington to Five Points

Two stories, told together: where the fraternity began, and how the Denver chapter grew into the Kappa Tower legacy.

Fraternity History

On January 5, 1911, ten students at Indiana University gave birth to a dream that became Kappa Alpha Psi — a brotherhood built on achievement in every field of human endeavor.

Denver (CO) Alumni Chapter History → Kappa Towers

Chartered February 20, 1954, the Denver Alumni Chapter has served the community for seven decades, culminating in the Kappa Tower housing developments that carry the chapter's mission into bricks and mortar.

1954

Denver Alumni Chapter chartered — the start of a legacy now carried forward through Kappa Tower I, II, and III.
