Who this is for

  • Brothers who were previously active with the Denver chapter
  • Brothers whose membership lapsed and want to reactivate
  • Brothers returning to Denver after time away

How It Works

  1. Submit the form below. Tell us when you were last active and how to reach you.
  2. Standing review. A chapter officer will look up your record and confirm your status.
  3. Reinstatement. We will walk you through any outstanding dues or paperwork.
  4. Welcome back. Attend a meeting and reconnect with your line and lineage.
