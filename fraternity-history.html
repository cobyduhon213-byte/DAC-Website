Fraternity History

Where Kappa Alpha Psi began, and the name it started under.

← History Denver Alumni History Kappa Tower Fraternity History Founders & Objectives Middle Western Province

Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. was founded on January 5, 1911, at Indiana University in Bloomington, by ten students seeking something the campus didn't offer them: real Black fellowship and a path to honorable achievement. What started as Kappa Alpha Nu became Kappa Alpha Psi in 1915, and has since grown into undergraduate and alumni chapters across the country.
