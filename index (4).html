The Denver Alumni Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. — serving the Denver community since our chartering on February 20, 1954.

We hold to a simple belief: brotherhood isn't just about looking out for each other — it's about looking out for the community around us. That belief shapes everything below, from holiday food drives to a scholarship fund that has now carried hundreds of young men through college.

Our legacy

  1. 1911

    Ten students at Indiana University in Bloomington found Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. on the night of January 5th.

  2. 1954

    The Denver Alumni Chapter is chartered on February 20th, with community service as its focus from day one.

  3. 1978

    Chapter members begin acquiring land at 2100 Downing Street, laying the groundwork for affordable senior housing.

  4. 1984

    Kappa Tower opens — a six-story, 45-unit residence for low-income seniors, still standing at 2160 Downing Street.

  5. 1987

    The Denver Kappa Alpha Psi Scholarship Foundation is chartered, earning federal 501(c)(3) status in 1989.

What we do

  • Holiday basket giveaways

    Food baskets for families across metro Denver each Thanksgiving and Christmas.

  • Guide Right & Kappa League

    Mentoring for middle and high school students — academics, career development, leadership.

  • Scholarship support

    Fundraising and volunteer hours behind the Scholarship Foundation's annual awards.

  • Affordable senior housing

    Kappa Housing Inc. and Kappa Management Inc. keep Kappa Tower running for Denver's elderly community.

Denver Kappa Alpha Psi Scholarship Foundation

Every year, the Foundation puts scholarship money — and, for qualifying students, a laptop — behind young Black men in Denver who've excelled in high school and are headed to college.
