Denver Kappa Alpha Psi Scholarship Foundation

The Foundation was formed to provide perpetual support for young African-American men who've excelled in high school and are headed to college — funding scholarships, honoring achievement, and promoting good citizenship. Every recipient also gets a laptop for college.
