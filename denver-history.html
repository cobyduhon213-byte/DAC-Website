Denver Alumni History

Seven decades of brotherhood and community impact in Denver.

← History Denver Alumni History Kappa Tower Fraternity History Founders & Objectives Middle Western Province

The Denver Alumni Chapter has spent seven decades turning brotherhood into brick-and-mortar community impact — starting with a charter in 1954 and, thirty years later, a building that's still housing Denver seniors today.

  1. 1954

    The Denver Alumni Chapter is chartered on February 20th, with community service as its focus from day one.

  2. 1978

    Chapter members begin acquiring land at 2100 Downing Street, laying the groundwork for affordable senior housing.

  3. 1984

    Kappa Tower opens — a six-story, 45-unit residence for low-income seniors. Full story on the Kappa Tower page.

  4. 1987

    The Denver Kappa Alpha Psi Scholarship Foundation is chartered, earning federal 501(c)(3) status in 1989.

  5. 2021

    Kappa Tower II opens in Denver's Northfield neighborhood, adding 70 more units of senior housing.
