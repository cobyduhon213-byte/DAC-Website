About us

The Denver Alumni Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. is a brotherhood of college-educated men committed to achievement, service, and mentorship across metro Denver — carrying forward a fraternity founded on January 5, 1911, at Indiana University.
