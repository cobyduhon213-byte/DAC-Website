The Denver Alumni Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. — serving the Denver community since our chartering on February 20, 1954.

We hold to a simple belief: brotherhood isn't just about looking out for each other — it's about looking out for the community around us. That belief shows up in a scholarship fund that's carried hundreds of young men through college, in a residence tower that's housed Denver seniors for four decades, and in the mentoring, giving, and fellowship below.

Kappa history & the home of Kappa Tower

Chartered February 20, 1954, the Denver Alumni Chapter has spent seven decades building toward something lasting — including Kappa Tower, the affordable senior housing chapter members founded in 1984 and have since expanded twice over.

About us

The Denver Alumni Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. is a brotherhood of college-educated men committed to achievement, service, and mentorship across metro Denver — carrying forward a fraternity founded on January 5, 1911, at Indiana University.
